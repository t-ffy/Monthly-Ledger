### New
- **Back up and restore.** Download all your data as one file and restore it on the same or a new device. The app checks the file and shows what it will replace before overwriting anything. A reminder appears if you haven't backed up in 30 days.
- **Edit anything.** Fix an income or expense entry, a savings goal or a contribution without deleting and re-adding it.
- **Repeating entries.** Tick **Repeat every month** for salary, rent or subscriptions. Each month they're ready to add with one tap, or skip.

### Fixes
- The app now tells you if the device's storage is full instead of failing silently.

**Live app:** https://t-ffy.github.io/Monthly-Ledger/
