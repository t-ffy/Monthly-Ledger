### New: your own categories
- **Add categories** on the new **Categories** page, or straight from the income or expense form with **+ New category**.
- **Rename** a category and the new name appears everywhere, including past entries, repeating items and budget limits.
- **Hide** categories you don't use. They leave the forms but stay on past entries.
- **Merge** two categories into one. Entries and repeating items move across, and budget limits are added together.
- **Delete** categories that have never been used.

### Under the hood
- Every category now has a permanent ID, so renaming or merging can never break existing entries. Your existing data is converted automatically the first time 1.4.0 opens.
- Backups now include your categories. Backups made with earlier versions still restore.

### Coming next
Version 1.5.0 will turn categories into accounts: income lands in **To assign**, you move money into each account, expenses come out of them, and balances roll over month to month. Savings goals become accounts too.
